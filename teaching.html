Teaching

I teach research methods and education policy at the University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education. Courses I taught earlier at the University of Massachusetts Amherst are listed on my CV.

Doctoral

  • Advanced Causal Inference Research and Reporting
    Doctoral seminar · Penn GSE · 2022, 2023

    An advanced methods course on estimating the causal effects of education policies and practices. Students learn randomized trials, matching, synthetic controls, fixed effects, difference-in-differences, and regression discontinuity, and apply them to real data in Stata through problem sets.

Master’s

  • Education Policy Research Practicum
    Penn GSE · 2023–2025

    Student teams partner with education leaders on a client-based research project. They carry out original data analysis, write a policy report, and present their findings to the client.

  • Economic Perspectives on Higher Education Policy
    Penn GSE · 2026

    How economists study higher education: access and admissions, financial aid and affordability, how institutions are financed, and the returns to college. Students apply economic reasoning to current policy debates through problem sets, a policy pitch, and a policy brief.

  • Education Policy Foundations
    Penn GSE · 2026

    A comparative introduction to primary and secondary education policy, including governance, school finance, school choice, curriculum, teachers, accountability, and implementation. Each student follows one U.S. state and one country and builds a comparative policy analysis.

  • Inquiry Fundamentals for Education Policy
    Summer Institute · Penn GSE · 2025–2026

    An introduction to the foundations of education research: quantitative and qualitative designs, sampling, measurement, statistics, research ethics, and program evaluation. A book club on major K–12 policy debates ties the methods to real questions.

For prospective students

My research is built on partnerships with school districts, state agencies, and college systems, such as the School District of Philadelphia and Virginia’s community colleges, to produce actionable research that informs policy and practice. I also serve as Faculty Research Director of the Office of School & Community Engagement.

I advise doctoral students and postdoctoral researchers and mentor undergraduate research assistants. If you are interested in this kind of work, please send me an email with a short description of your interests.
