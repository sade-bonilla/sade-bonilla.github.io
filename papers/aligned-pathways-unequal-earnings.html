Working paper, 2026

Aligned Pathways, Unequal Earnings: Gendered Transitions from Community College to Work

Bonilla, S. & Sparks, D.

EdWorkingPaper 26-1635, Annenberg Institute at Brown University

Working paper (DOI) Download PDF

Summary

This paper studies Virginia’s Get a Skill, Get a Job, Get Ahead (G3) initiative, which makes community college tuition-free for income-eligible students in high-demand workforce fields. Using linked college and earnings records for students who entered between 2016 and 2020, it compares workforce programs that later became G3-eligible with those that did not. Men in several designated fields, especially skilled trades and public safety, earn more after entry, while women see limited earnings gains and some employment declines. Employment in jobs aligned with the program rises for both women and men.
