Journal article, 2025

The Role of Policy Design in Free College Programs: Evidence from Virginia

Sparks, D. & Bonilla, S.

Education Finance and Policy

Publisher version

This study examines how the design of tuition-free college programs shapes institutional offerings and student enrollment, focusing on Virginia’s workforce-targeted G3 initiative. We find that both the number of eligible programs offered by community colleges and student enrollment in those programs increased by approximately 30% following implementation, while enrollment in transfer-oriented programs declined modestly. These descriptive findings highlight how targeted financial aid policies can shape institutional and student choices, with implications for access to longer-term educational pathways.

This is related to work on the Virginia G3 program.
